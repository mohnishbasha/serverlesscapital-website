Common Questions

Everything founders ask us.

The most common questions we get.

What stage does Serverless Capital invest at? +
Pre-seed. We typically lead with initial checks of $150K to $500K, then step up meaningfully in follow-ons with $1M to $10M.
What sectors does Serverless Capital focus on? +
AI infrastructure, agentic payments, and agentic commerce. If AI agents are central to what you are building, we want to hear from you.
Do you invest outside the US? +
Yes. We invest globally. We have offices in Austin, San Jose, and India but geography does not drive our decisions. Great founders are everywhere.
How long does your investment process take? +
One week from first call to decision. A quick intro, a deeper conversation, then a clear answer.
What do you look for in founders? +
Founders who know the problem deeply. Technical background helps, but insight matters more than credentials. We evaluate every team on its own merits.
Do you lead rounds? +
Yes. We lead pre-seed rounds. We also co-invest alongside other funds when that is what the round needs.
